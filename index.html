About

I am a Master's student in Data Science at EPFL and a Research Engineer at the LiGHT Lab, where I work on clinical large language models: supervised fine-tuning, preference tuning, and the analysis of how training changes a model's internal representations.

I hold a Bachelor's in Communication Systems from EPFL. My coursework and projects cover machine learning, NLP, foundation models, large-scale data and data visualization, and I have built LLM-based applications, deep learning models for computer vision and interactive data analyses.

I am interested in AI engineering and data science roles where models have to work reliably on real data and for real users.

Experience

Research Engineer, Clinical LLM Fine-tuning & Representation Analysis

Feb 2026 – Aug 2026

LiGHT Lab, EPFL, Lausanne

  • Fine-tuned Qwen3-32B on clinical Q&A data using Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and analyzed the effect of subsequent preference tuning (DPO / Offset DPO) on internal model representations.
  • Built an end-to-end hidden-state extraction and linear probing pipeline across 8 model checkpoints on a 4×A100 HPC cluster.
  • Ran cosine similarity, PCA geometry and logistic probe classifiers on paired chosen/rejected representations across 8 transformer layers.
